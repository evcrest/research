# Everest

**Research. Capital. Ventures.**

Independent public-equity research, investment intelligence and selected venture-building work by **Evans Jep**.

Everest is an independent research and venture platform. This repository is its public research archive: a record of questions investigated, evidence reviewed, analytical methods, conclusions, uncertainty and subsequent updates.

## Editorial mandate

Everest publishes decision-useful work grounded in primary evidence. The objective is not constant market commentary or manufactured conviction. It is to improve investment decisions through disciplined research.

### Principles

- **Evidence before opinion:** prioritize audited accounts, regulatory filings, exchange notices and company releases.
- **Valuation matters:** a good business is not automatically a good investment at every price.
- **Separate fact from interpretation:** distinguish reported data, estimates, assumptions and judgment.
- **Show the downside:** identify thesis breakers, balance-sheet risks and liquidity constraints.
- **Respect uncertainty:** state data gaps and confidence limits.
- **Keep an audit trail:** date research, link sources and preserve material revisions.
- **No false precision:** do not publish targets or return forecasts without a transparent basis.

## Coverage

The initial universe is Nigeria-first, with international securities considered when access, availability, costs and risks can be evidenced.

- Nigerian listed equities and sector analysis
- International companies and diversified funds
- Earnings quality, financial statements and capital allocation
- Valuation, catalysts and downside scenarios
- Market structure, investor access and transaction costs
- Research tools, datasets and selected technology ventures

## Publication standard

Every substantive note should state its publication date and information cut-off, scope and horizon, key conclusion, evidence and source links, valuation context where relevant, principal risks, thesis-break conditions, data limitations and applicable disclosures.

A watchlist or research priority is not a buy recommendation. A company can be operationally strong and still be overvalued.

## Publishing workflow

1. Define the research question and information cut-off.
2. Collect primary sources and record publication dates.
3. Reconcile figures, periods, units and currencies.
4. Test the thesis against downside cases and rejection criteria.
5. Draft the full research note before adapting it for social channels.
6. Run factual, numerical, source-link and disclosure checks.
7. Publish with a date and version; update transparently when evidence changes.

Short-form content must preserve material caveats and link to the full note.
