# Nigeria's 2026 equity rally calls for selectivity, not shortcuts

### An initial Nigeria-first research screen — and the questions that still need answering

**By Evans Jep | Everest**  
**12 November 2026**

Nigeria's equity market has delivered a powerful advance in 2026. NGX Group reported the All-Share Index up 62.23% year to date as of 10 November.

That number does not tell us which companies are undervalued, whether earnings can sustain the rally, or whether a new investor should buy today. It is a reminder that price, expectations and risk must be examined together.

I have started with a Nigeria-first screen: local listed companies form the core universe, while international securities are considered only where a plausible access route for a Nigerian investor has been identified. The result is a queue for further work — not a portfolio and not a set of trade instructions.

## Four companies for deeper diligence

### GTCO: strong capital metrics, but earnings need normalization

The Q3 2026 company release cited in the screen reports nine-month PBT of ₦879.9bn, total assets of ₦18.37tn, group capital adequacy of 34.2% and Stage 3 loans of 4.0%. PBT was down 2.3% year on year.

The next step is to understand earnings composition, impairment coverage, capital deployment, dividend capacity and the valuation investors are paying. Capital strength is useful; it is not a substitute for normalized earnings analysis.

### Zenith Bank: test resilience and the dividend

Zenith Bank belongs in the deeper-work queue because its scale makes earnings resilience and dividend sustainability worth examining. The work still required is specific: updated statements, normalized ROE, non-performing loans, capital adequacy, dividend cover and peer valuation.

### Dangote Cement: growth is visible; expectations matter

The Q3 2026 release reports revenue of ₦1,260.3bn (+16.4% y/y), EBITDA of ₦578.0bn (+19.6%) and PAT of ₦336.3bn (+50.9%).

Those are strong reported growth rates. They must be considered alongside volumes, margins, energy costs, FX exposure, capex, net debt and the share-price advance already recorded. The central question is how much improvement is already reflected in valuation.

### Seplat Energy: cash generation with commodity and operating exposure

Seplat's nine-month 2026 results cited in the screen report free cash flow of US$749m and average working-interest production of 144,999 boepd, 7% higher year on year.

Cash generation can support balance-sheet repair and shareholder distributions. But it is exposed to realized commodity prices, production reliability, operating conditions, security and capital requirements. Stress-test those drivers rather than extrapolate one period's cash flow.

## Four names remain on the watchlist

Airtel Africa reported constant-currency revenue growth of 20.8% and Nigeria revenue growth of 24.4% for the half year to 30 September 2026. The next questions concern FX translation, leverage, network investment, cash conversion and valuation.

UBA offers pan-African banking exposure, but the analysis must account for country risk, currency effects, credit quality and subsidiary capital requirements.

MTN Nigeria merits monitoring for data demand and potential earnings recovery. Debt, FX exposure, capex, regulation and actual free-cash-flow recovery remain central tests.

BUA Foods provides consumer-staples exposure. Input costs, purchasing power, working capital, margins and valuation determine whether that exposure is attractive at a given price.

## International exposure: access is only the first question

The initial international watchlist includes VOO and VTI as broad US-market funds, alongside Microsoft, Apple and NVIDIA. These instruments are not interchangeable: diversified funds and individual companies carry different concentration and business risks.

The source review identified Bamboo as a potential route for a Nigerian investor and found a relevant SEC Nigeria directory entry. That does not guarantee current product availability or settle custody, transferability, investor protection, fees or taxes. Each must be checked against current official documentation and the actual account before funding.

## The discipline for a small starting balance

A small account is a reason to pay closer attention to friction. Keep emergency reserves and near-term spending money outside volatile equities. Avoid leverage, CFDs and options while capital and process are developing. FX spreads, commissions, statutory charges, custody and withdrawal costs can make small transactions uneconomic. Diversification should be assessed by underlying business and risk exposure, not merely by ticker count.

## What this screen does not claim

The screen does not establish fair value or attractive entry prices. Current official market prices and liquidity, normalized peer multiples, updated filings for several companies, and a verified broker-cost and custody assessment remain outstanding. Personal contribution schedule, liquidity needs, time horizon, drawdown tolerance, debt and emergency savings would also be necessary before considering portfolio weights.

The next stage is deeper company work: reconcile earnings, build valuation cases, stress-test downside scenarios and identify the evidence that would invalidate each thesis. A research priority can be removed as readily as advanced.

The objective is not a long list of stocks. It is a repeatable process for deciding what deserves capital — and what does not.
