# Everest Research and Publication Standard

**Owner:** Evans Jep | **Applies to:** company notes, screens, valuation work, social posts and public datasets.

## 1. Scope

Begin with a precise research question. Record the issuer or instrument, geography, period, intended reader, horizon and information cut-off.

## 2. Source hierarchy

Prefer, where relevant: (1) audited statements and regulatory filings; (2) exchange notices and company releases; (3) regulators and government publications; (4) investor presentations and transcripts; (5) reputable market-data providers; (6) news reporting and specialist commentary. Community discussion is qualitative context, not proof.

## 3. Evidence discipline

For each material figure, retain the URL, document title, publication date, period, units, currency and page or section. Distinguish reported data, management guidance, third-party estimates, Everest calculations, assumptions and judgment. Reconcile periods, currencies and group-versus-company figures. Flag restatements and inconsistencies.

## 4. Analytical minimum

Assess the relevant subset of business model, segment economics, revenue quality, margins, cash conversion, earnings normalization, balance sheet, liquidity, debt maturity, capital allocation, dilution, governance, regulation, valuation, catalysts, downside cases, thesis breakers, liquidity and currency risks.

For banks, add capital adequacy, asset quality, provisioning, funding and normalized ROE. For commodity businesses, stress-test price, volume, costs, capex and operating exposure.

## 5. Valuation

Use methods appropriate to the business. State metric, period, share count, net-debt treatment, currency, assumptions and date. If data are insufficient, say so; do not fabricate a target. Scenarios must identify drivers and avoid false precision.

## 6. Thesis quality

Every conclusion should address: what may be misunderstood; what evidence supports the view; what is already priced in; what disproves the thesis; the downside if wrong; and what remains unknown.

A high-quality business is not automatically an attractive security. Price and opportunity cost matter.

## 7. Publication controls

Check dates, cut-off, numerical claims, units, currencies, links, distinction between fact and opinion, counterarguments, risks, disclosures, version and correction path. Social derivatives must preserve material caveats.

## 8. Corrections

Correct typographical errors transparently. For material factual or analytical changes, update the version/date and add a change log explaining what changed and why. Preserve superseded versions where practical.

## 9. Independence

Disclose relevant holdings, compensation, commercial relationships, sponsorships and other conflicts when applicable. Do not imply regulated status, institutional affiliation or audited performance unless accurate and substantiated.
